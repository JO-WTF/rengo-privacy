Galplay 支持

Galplay 是在 iPhone 与 iPad 上运行用户自行导入的 Ren'Py、Kirikiri 与 ONScripter 视觉小说的播放器。有任何问题， 欢迎邮件联系：zhongzhyu@gmail.com

常见问题

怎么把作品导入进来？

把完整游戏目录或 ZIP / 7z / RAR 归档通过「文件」App、AirDrop 或 App 内的「导入游戏」加入 Galplay； 也可以主动开启局域网浏览器传输。Ren'Py、Kirikiri 与 ONScripter 的项目结构各不相同， 请保留原游戏所需的脚本和资源，不要只导入其中一个文件。

画面上下有大片黑边？

多数视觉小说采用横屏比例，竖屏握持时画面可能只占据屏幕中间一部分，这是正常的等比缩放。 把设备转成横屏即可全屏显示；也可以在「设置 › 游戏体验」中开启锁定横屏， 让作品直接以横屏呈现。

存档能备份或换设备吗？

可以。「设置 › 资料与存储 › 存档备份」支持备份到 iCloud Drive 等外部文件夹或你自建的 WebDAV 服务器。 换设备时也可以把作品连同存档打包导出，通过 AirDrop 传到另一台设备。

WebDAV 存档备份已经提供；从 WebDAV 或网盘直接导入游戏仍在开发中。

作品跑不起来 / 报错？

Galplay 内置 17 个 Ren'Py 运行时，覆盖 7.4.11 至 8.5.3，并支持 Kirikiri 和 ONScripter。 App 会依据项目入口和资源准备启动方案；有些作品需要在作品设置中手动选择入口或 Ren'Py 运行时。 不同作品的资源格式与所需组件不同，部分作品目前可能无法运行。仍然报错时，请附作品名称、引擎、所选入口及错误信息联系支持。

Galplay 提供游戏内容吗？

不提供。除内置示例作品外，Galplay 不提供或分发第三方游戏。 你导入的内容由你自行获取，其来源与适龄性由你负责。

Galplay Support

Galplay plays visual novels you import on iPhone and iPad using Ren'Py, Kirikiri, or ONScripter. For any question, email zhongzhyu@gmail.com.

How do I import a work?

Import a complete game folder or a ZIP / 7z / RAR archive using Files, AirDrop, or "Import Game" in Galplay. You can also start a local-network browser transfer. Keep the original scripts and resources; Ren'Py, Kirikiri, and ONScripter use different project layouts.

Why are there black bars above and below?

Many visual novels use a landscape aspect ratio. Held in portrait, the picture may fill only a band across the middle — that is correct scaling, not a fault. Turn the device to landscape, or enable landscape lock under Settings › Gameplay.

Can I back up or move my saves?

Yes. Settings › Data & Storage › Save Backup supports an external folder such as iCloud Drive or your own WebDAV server. You can export a work together with its saves and AirDrop it to another device.

WebDAV save backup is available now; direct game import from WebDAV or cloud storage is still in development.

Why does a game fail to start?

Galplay includes 17 Ren'Py runtimes covering versions 7.4.11–8.5.3 and supports Kirikiri and ONScripter. It selects a launch configuration from the game's entry point and resources. Some games need a manually chosen entry or Ren'Py runtime; some resource formats and components are not supported yet. Please include the game name, engine, selected entry, and error details when contacting support.

Does Galplay supply games?

No. Apart from the bundled sample, Galplay does not provide or distribute third-party games. You obtain imported content yourself and are responsible for its source and age suitability.
